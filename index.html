HND in Computing (Cyber Security)

Secure the Digital Future — From Ethical Hacking to AI-Powered Defense, You'll Protect the Digital Economy.

Overview of the Course

From protecting global businesses against cyberattacks to securing sensitive cloud data, cybersecurity professionals are in high demand. With our HND in Cybersecurity, you can be the expert behind these critical defenses!

Build the Future — Secure the Digital World

From Ethical Hacking to AI-Powered Defense, You'll Protect the Digital Economy.

What You'll Learn:

  • Ethical Hacking & Threat Defense — Learn to identify and neutralize cyber threats like those faced by Sony and Equifax.
  • Network Security & Encryption — Gain hands-on experience with firewalls and encryption, just as banks and healthcare systems do to protect sensitive data.
  • Cloud Security & AI-Driven Defense — Master security techniques used by tech giants like Google and Amazon to safeguard their infrastructure.
  • Industry-Standard Tools — Train with Kali Linux, AWS, Cisco, and more to prepare for real-world cybersecurity challenges.

Why Choose Cybersecurity?

  • Be the Protector of the Digital World — Secure networks, data, and digital assets from ever-evolving threats.
  • Unlock High-Paying Job Opportunities — Cybersecurity professionals earn $90,000+ globally, with demand growing rapidly.
  • Master AI-Powered Defense Systems — Learn penetration testing, incident response, and threat intelligence to stay ahead of cybercriminals.
  • Work Across Multiple Sectors — From Google & Microsoft to healthcare, finance, and government, cybersecurity experts are needed everywhere.

Why Choose Us?

  • Globally Recognized Cybersecurity Certification
  • Hands-on practical training with industry tools
  • Experienced faculty with real-world expertise
  • Pathway to top international universities

HND in Business (Management)

Lead, Innovate & Succeed! — From Leadership to Strategy, You'll Power the Business World.

Overview of the Course

Ready to take your business career to the next level? Our HND in Business Management equips you with the skills to lead teams, make strategic decisions, and drive business success in any industry. Whether you aspire to be a manager, entrepreneur, or corporate leader, this program provides the foundation for a thriving career!

Build the Future — Lead, Innovate & Succeed!

From Leadership to Strategy, You'll Power the Business World.

What You'll Learn:

  • Leadership & Management — Develop the skills to manage teams and businesses effectively.
  • Strategic Business Planning — Learn how to create and implement successful business strategies.
  • Marketing & Branding — Master modern marketing techniques to promote businesses.
  • Finance & Business Operations — Gain insights into financial management, budgeting, and business analytics.

Why Choose Business Management?

  • High-Demand Skills — Management professionals are needed in every industry.
  • Unlock Global Career Opportunities — Work in corporate, government, or entrepreneurial roles.
  • Pathway to a Degree — Progress to a Bachelor's in Business Administration (BBA).
  • Hands-On Learning — Apply theories through case studies, simulations, and real-world projects.

Why Choose Us?

  • Globally Recognized & Accredited Program
  • Industry-experienced faculty members
  • Strong industry connections and internship opportunities
  • Pathway to top international universities

About Us

Welcome to ABC Higher Education Institute — A premier institution bringing world-class UK qualifications to Sri Lanka.

Welcome to ABC Higher Education Institute

Welcome to ABC Higher Education Institute, located in Colombo, a premier higher education institution committed to fostering academic excellence, industry-ready skills, and global perspectives. We bring world-class UK qualifications to Sri Lanka, making quality international education accessible to local students.

With world-class facilities, internationally recognized programmes, and a vibrant multicultural community, we prepare students to thrive in a competitive world and empower them to launch careers or ventures locally or globally.

Our HND Computing and Business programmes provide direct pathways to top international universities, making quality UK education accessible to Sri Lankan students. We also offer a BSc programme in Information Technology for those seeking a full degree qualification.
